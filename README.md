# nemoclaw-louis
Most AI assistants help us work. "Louis" helps us live my free time at 110%.

Louis is a long-running, multi-agent leisure concierge built with NVIDIA NemoClaw and powered by NVIDIA APIs.

Its Explorer discovers sports, culture, museums, and local experiences. The Planner checks calendar availability. The Skeptic learns from feedback. The Serendipity Guardian introduces something genuinely new, while the Auditor blocks repetitive, impractical, or low-confidence proposals.

Louis does not simply recommend. It continuously searches, debates, plans, learns, and improves. I want him to keep multi-memory and following my taste wo being FOMO!

In the future, "Louis" could collaborate with museums, sports clubs, tourist offices, venues, and hospitality partners to surface relevant offers, including underbooked time slots and last-minute experiences.

I want to save preparation time, getting the exclusive experiences, and ... partners could reach qualified audiences, and Louis can generate revenue through transparent commissions, premium subscriptions, or concierge services.

"Louis" transforms free time into meaningful, fully cultural moments!
